## What it is

Benedict Evans is an independent tech analyst who sends a weekly newsletter on the most important news of the week and what it means. A paid tier adds more commentary.

## What we like

- **Perspective.** It explains why a story matters, not just that it happened.
- **Big-picture thinking.** Strong on platforms, business models and long-term shifts.
- **Brevity.** Short notes on each item, easy to read in one sitting.

## What to know

- **One person's view.** Thoughtful, but it's analysis, not neutral reporting.
- **Weekly.** It won't keep you current day to day.

## Who it suits

Product people, founders and investors who want to understand where tech is going. For the daily news underneath, use a brief like [ReadKernel](/).
