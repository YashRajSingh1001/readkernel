## What it is

Import AI is a weekly newsletter by Jack Clark, a co-founder of Anthropic, covering notable AI research papers and what they might mean for the world. Each issue walks through a handful of papers with commentary, and it often ends with a short piece of fiction.

## What we like

- **Research, explained.** It picks papers you'd otherwise miss and says why they matter.
- **A clear voice.** You get an informed opinion, not just a summary.
- **Policy awareness.** It connects technical results to safety and policy questions.

## What to know

- **It's opinionated by design.** Read it as one well-informed perspective.
- **Not for breaking news.** It won't tell you which model launched this morning.

## Who it suits

Anyone who wants to understand where AI research is heading, not just what shipped. ReadKernel reads Import AI as one of its [sources](/sources), so notable issues can show up in the brief.
