## What it is

The Pragmatic Engineer is a newsletter by Gergely Orosz about software engineering and the tech industry: how teams work, how companies hire and pay, and what's changing in engineering practice. There is a free edition and a paid subscription with more in-depth articles.

## What we like

- **Depth.** Long, well-researched pieces you can't get from news coverage.
- **Insider detail.** Real numbers and real examples from inside tech companies.
- **Useful for careers.** Good reading for engineers and managers thinking about their next step.

## What to know

- **The best pieces are paid.** The free edition is useful, but it's a taste.
- **It's not a news feed.** Expect a few big pieces, not daily updates.

## Who it suits

Engineers and engineering managers who want to understand the industry they work in. Pair it with a daily brief like [ReadKernel](/) for the news itself.
