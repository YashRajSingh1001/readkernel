## What it is

TLDR is a free daily email that rounds up tech news, startup stories and programming links, each with a few lines of summary. Beyond the main edition, it runs separate editions for specific areas such as AI and web development, so you can pick the ones that match your work.

## What we like

- **Breadth.** You see a lot of what happened in a couple of minutes.
- **Separate editions.** Developers can get a developer-focused email instead of everything.
- **Consistent format.** Every item looks the same, which makes it easy to scan.

## What to know

- **It's link-first.** Items are short, so you'll click out for detail on anything important.
- **Sponsored slots.** Like most free newsletters, it carries clearly marked sponsor sections.
- **Several editions can add up.** Subscribing to many at once brings back the inbox overload you were trying to escape.

## Who it suits

People who want a broad daily scan and are happy to click through. If you'd rather have duplicate coverage merged into one story with every source attached, that's what [ReadKernel](/) does.
