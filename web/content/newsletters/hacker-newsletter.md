## What it is

Hacker Newsletter is a free weekly email that picks the best links and discussions from Hacker News, grouped by theme. It has been running for many years and is curated by a person, not an algorithm.

## What we like

- **Curation.** Fewer, better links than the front page on any given day.
- **Weekly rhythm.** One email, no daily guilt.
- **Discussion links.** It points to the comment threads, which is often where the real insight is.

## What to know

- **It's not news.** By the time it arrives, the stories are days old. That's the point, but it won't keep you current.
- **Hacker News taste.** Expect programming, startups and science, with the community's particular interests.

## Who it suits

Developers who want the best of Hacker News once a week. For what developers are discussing *today*, see ReadKernel's [Top articles](/feed/top).
