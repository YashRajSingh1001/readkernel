Most global tech briefings barely mention India. If you work in Indian tech, invest here, or are building a startup, you need both views: what's happening globally, and what's happening at home.

## What to follow

- **Funding and exits.** Who raised, how much, from whom, and the steady flow of new-age tech IPOs.
- **Company results.** Listed startups now report every quarter, and their numbers move the whole sector's mood.
- **Policy and regulation.** Data protection, payments, digital competition and sector rules often matter more than any single funding round.
- **Big Tech in India.** Data centres, local manufacturing, and the market share fights in payments and commerce.

## Where to read it

ReadKernel reads several Indian sources every morning, including Inc42, YourStory, ET Tech, Moneycontrol and Mint's technology section. They're listed on our [sources page](/sources). For deeper analysis, paid publications like The Ken and The Morning Context are worth considering if you need to go further.

## How to keep it to one read

1. **One morning brief for everything.** ReadKernel's brief mixes global and Indian stories by importance, and it lands at 6 AM IST.
2. **Startups as its own topic.** Open the [Startups feed](/feed/startups) for the full day's funding and company news.
3. **Make it yours.** If India's startup scene is your main interest, [build a brief](/brief/customize) with Startups and Markets only.

## A word on numbers

Indian startup coverage often quotes figures in crore (₹1 crore = ₹10 million). ReadKernel keeps figures as the sources report them, so check the unit before comparing a ₹ number with a $ one.
